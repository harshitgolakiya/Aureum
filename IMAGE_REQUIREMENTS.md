# Aureum Website — Image Size Guide

## Export settings

- Format: JPG or WebP
- Colour profile: sRGB
- Quality: 85–90%
- Do not add text, logos, borders or watermarks to images.
- Keep the main subject away from the outer 15% so images can crop safely on mobile.

2. Main exterior

5. Construction, engineering or material detail
6. Human-scale or dusk view
